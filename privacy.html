VYRO STUDIO / INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

Last updated: 18 November 2026

1. Who this notice covers

This notice describes the intended handling of information when you visit the VYRO Studio website or contact the studio through its linked Facebook Page. The responsible data controller/operator details must be confirmed by the adult legally responsible for the studio before this notice is treated as final.

2. Information this static website collects

The current GitHub Pages website does not include an account system, checkout, client intake form, analytics tracker or advertising pixel. It does not intentionally ask visitors to provide personal information directly on the website. GitHub Pages and the visitor's browser may process limited technical information needed to deliver and secure the site; consult GitHub's current privacy documentation for its hosting practices.

If you contact VYRO through Facebook, the information you choose to send (such as your name, profile information and message) is processed by Meta and may also be seen by the people managing the Page. Avoid sending sensitive personal information, passwords, card details or identity documents.

3. Why information may be used

Messages may be used to respond to questions, assess project inquiries, prepare a quote and communicate about a potential project. Information should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for that purpose. Do not use inquiry information for unrelated marketing without an appropriate legal basis and notice.

4. Sharing and service providers

The site is hosted using GitHub Pages and inquiries are routed to Facebook. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. VYRO does not sell visitor personal information. No additional analytics or advertising services are intentionally installed in this website version.

5. Retention and security

Messages should be retained only as long as reasonably needed for the inquiry, project administration or a legal requirement, then securely deleted where appropriate. Access to inquiry messages and project files should be limited to authorized people. Do not send confidential files through public links without checking access permissions.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, objection or other action concerning personal information. To make a request, contact the VYRO Studio Facebook Page and identify the message or information concerned. The responsible adult must verify the request and respond in accordance with applicable law, including the Philippine Data Privacy Act where applicable.

7. Children and minors

This website is not designed to collect personal information from children. If you are under 18, do not send private or sensitive information. A parent or guardian should be involved in any business inquiry or agreement involving a minor.

8. Changes and contact

This notice may be updated as the website or services change. The current version will be posted here. Contact VYRO through its Facebook Page for privacy questions.

Important: This page is an initial website notice, not legal advice or a substitute for a lawyer's review. It must be checked and approved by the responsible adult/legal guardian and, where appropriate, a qualified Philippine privacy or business professional before VYRO accepts paid client work.
