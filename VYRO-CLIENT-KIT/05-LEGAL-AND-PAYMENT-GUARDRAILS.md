# VYRO Studio — Adult Review Required

This is a preparation checklist, not legal or financial advice.

Before accepting any client payment or signing any agreement:
- A parent/legal guardian must supervise the business and payment arrangements, including provider eligibility and account ownership.
- Do not misstate age, identity, business registration or account ownership.
- Have a parent/guardian review the client agreement, privacy notice, cancellation/refund terms, intellectual-property terms and tax/registration obligations.
- If needed, obtain advice from a qualified Philippine professional before using the documents with paying clients.
- Do not collect card numbers, passwords, government ID images or unnecessary personal information in the website inquiry form.
- Do not place payment provider secret keys, API credentials or database secrets in this public static website.

## Current technical status
The GitHub Pages website is static. It does not have secure checkout, server-side form handling, authentication, database storage or a payment webhook. The inquiry page only formats the visitor's answers for manual copying into Messenger. No payment is collected by the website.
