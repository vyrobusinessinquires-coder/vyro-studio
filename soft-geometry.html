Soft

A study of volume, surface and light. Simple geometric forms become a visual language through proportion, material contrast and careful composition.

STUDY SERIES / 2026INDEPENDENT EXPLORATION

01 / THE INTENT

Quiet forms.
Clear presence.

Soft Geometry explores the balance between precise geometry and softer, tactile surfaces. The work studies how a small number of forms can create depth without visual noise.

Rather than relying on decorative effects, the direction is built through silhouette, scale, light and the relationship between objects.
